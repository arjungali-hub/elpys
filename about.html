About Elpys

A curated directory of verified volunteer opportunities for teens in the Bellevue area — with exact sign-up steps, no broken links, and nothing you have to figure out.

Why this exists

This started with a King County Library System list of teen volunteer opportunities. It had 13 organizations on it — but the information was years out of date, and almost every link just went to an organization's homepage instead of an actual signup page. Age requirements were buried or missing entirely. Some of the programs on it had closed, or started charging a fee.

That's the whole reason Elpys exists: a directory where every listing has actually been checked, so you can go from "I want to volunteer" to "I signed up" in one visit — no dead ends, no digging through an organization's website trying to figure out if they even take teens.

What "verified" actually means

Every listing on Elpys is checked against that organization's own page before it goes live — not a search result, not an old list, their actual current signup process. Every listing has to clear the same bar:

  • No adult has to be there with you (a waiver you sign at home is fine — a parent standing next to you the whole time is not)
  • The sign-up steps are clear enough that you can actually complete them
  • Roughly 20 minutes of Bellevue
  • Free — no program fees

If an organization requires a long application and interview process, a background check with a long wait, or isn't really an opportunity you can sign up for (just a directory pointing elsewhere), it doesn't get listed.

Separately from whether the opportunity works for a teen, we check that the organization is a real, registered one — a government body or a charity that's current with the IRS and Washington State. How we check organizations walks through exactly what that involves, and what it doesn't cover.

The name

Elpys comes from Elpis, the Greek personification of hope — the one thing left in Pandora's box after everything else got out. "Sign Up. Show Up." is the idea behind it: hope is more useful when it comes with an actual next step.

Who's behind it

Elpys is built and maintained by one person — a student in Bellevue who got frustrated with that KCLS list and decided to fix it. It's still early: new listings get added regularly, and the goal is to keep expanding to nearby cities over time.

Help make it better

Know an organization that should be listed? Found something wrong with a listing — a broken link, an outdated detail? Both take less than a minute to send in, and both directly shape what's on the site.
