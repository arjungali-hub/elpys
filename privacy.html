Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026

Elpys ("we," "us") runs elpys.vercel.app, a directory of verified volunteer opportunities for teens in the Bellevue, WA area. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what choices you have about it. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, email us (see "Contact" below) and ask.

Elpys is meant for use by teens age 13 and older. If you're younger than that, please don't create an account or submit information to us.

1. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the site:

If you...We collect
Just browse listingsNothing that identifies you personally — see "Analytics" below.
Create an accountYour email address and password (your password is handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store it in plain text).
Set interests/availability for the weekly digestThe categories and days/times you select, tied to your account. Both are optional and editable any time in your account settings.
Submit an opportunity for listingWhatever you enter in the form — the organization's details, and your own contact info if you choose to include it in the "notes for reviewer" field.
Send feedback about a listingThe feedback text you write, plus which listing it's about.

2. How we use it

  • Account & login: to let you log in and manage your account.
  • Weekly digest email: to send you new opportunities matching the interests and availability you've set — only if you have an account with an email on file and haven't unsubscribed.
  • Submissions & feedback: to review and, where accurate, publish new listings, and to fix or flag problems with existing ones. A real person reviews every submission before it goes live — nothing is published automatically.

We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising — Elpys doesn't run ads.

3. Analytics

We use a small amount of anonymous, aggregate analytics (page views and a few specific actions, like using a filter or clicking a signup link) to understand which parts of the site are actually useful, so we can improve it. This is configured to avoid identifying you personally: no cookies or persistent tracking identifiers are used for analytics, session recording is turned off, and analytics data is never linked to your account, email, or anything you type into a form.

4. Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information. A small number of service providers process data on our behalf, only for the purpose of running the site:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account login/authentication.
  • Vercel — hosts the website itself.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile) — a spam-prevention check on our submission and feedback forms; it processes some technical information (like your browser and a temporary token) to confirm you're a real visitor, not a bot.
  • Google (Gmail) — sends the weekly digest email, if you've signed up for one.
  • PostHog — provides the anonymous analytics described above.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

5. Cookies & local storage

Logging in uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in — this is required for accounts to work and isn't used for tracking. Our spam-prevention check (Cloudflare Turnstile) sets its own short-lived verification cookie when you submit a form. Our analytics is configured not to use cookies at all.

6. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the weekly digest any time — every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also turn it off from your account page.
  • Edit or clear your interests and availability any time from your account page.
  • Delete your account entirely, any time, from your account page — this permanently removes your account and profile data.

7. Children's privacy

Elpys is intended for teens age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we've collected information from a child under 13, we'll delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since Elpys is a small, actively developed project, check back occasionally.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data? Email elpysnotifications@gmail.com.

Listings are checked regularly for accuracy, but links and details can change — always confirm with the org before your first visit. Something look wrong? Let us know.
