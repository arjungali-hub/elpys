Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

Elpys ("we," "us") runs elpys.vercel.app — a directory of verified volunteer opportunities for teens in the Bellevue, WA area. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, who else ends up seeing it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It's written in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email us (see "Contact" at the bottom) and ask.

Elpys is built for teens age 13 and older. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them — see section 11.

The short version

  • You can browse the entire site without an account and without telling us anything about yourself.
  • An account needs one thing: an email address. Everything else is optional.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, and Elpys doesn't run ads.
  • Our analytics is anonymous — no cookies, no session recording, no profile of you.
  • You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, yourself, at any time.

The rest of this page is the detail behind those five points.

2. How we use it

  • Account and login: to let you log in and manage your account.
  • Weekly digest email: to send you new opportunities matching the interests and availability you've set — only if you have an account with an email on file and haven't unsubscribed. Every digest includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Submissions and feedback: to review and, where accurate, publish new listings, and to fix or flag problems with existing ones. A real person reviews every submission before it goes live — nothing is published automatically.
  • Replying to you: if you email us, or give us an email address with your feedback, we use it to answer.
  • Keeping the site working and honest: spam prevention, rate limiting, security, and the anonymous usage counts described in section 5.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never have.
  • We do not share it for advertising of any kind, including cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising. Elpys doesn't run ads.
  • We do not share it with data brokers, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • We do not build advertising or behavioral profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not record your screen, your typing, or your mouse movements.
  • We do not add people who submit an opportunity or send feedback to any mailing list.

4. Cookies and local storage

Elpys sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Three things do use your browser's storage:

  • Staying logged in: if you have an account, your login session is kept in your browser's local storage. This is what makes accounts work; it isn't used for tracking, and it goes away when you log out.
  • The beta banner: once you dismiss it, that fact is stored for the rest of your browsing session so it doesn't reappear on every page.
  • Spam prevention: Cloudflare Turnstile sets its own short-lived verification cookie when you submit one of our forms.

Our analytics is configured to use no cookies and no persistent identifiers at all.

5. Analytics

We use a small amount of anonymous, aggregate analytics — through PostHog — to understand which parts of the site are actually useful, so we can improve them. What's recorded:

  • Page views (which page, when).
  • Two specific actions: clicking a listing's sign-up button, and successfully submitting the opportunity form or the feedback form. The event records only that the action happened — never what you typed.
  • Basic page-performance measurements (how fast pages load), and technical details your browser sends with any web request, such as the browser and device type and an approximate city-level location derived from your IP address. PostHog receives your IP address to make that estimate; it is not stored against a profile, because no profile exists.

It is deliberately configured for privacy: no cookies or persistent identifiers, no session or screen recording, no blanket click tracking, and no identified user profiles — so nothing here can be tied to your account, your email, or a previous visit. Using the filters, the age box, or the map is not tracked. We can see that a page was viewed 40 times; we cannot see that you viewed it.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we honor it. We don't sell or share personal information in any case, so there's nothing for it to switch off — but we won't override it either.

7. How long we keep things

We don't keep personal information indefinitely. Specifically:

8. Your choices and your rights

You can do all of this yourself, any time:

  • Unsubscribe from the weekly digest — every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also turn it off from your account page.
  • Edit or clear your interests and availability from your account page.
  • Delete your account from your account page. This permanently removes your account and its profile record — email address, interests, availability — and stops all digest emails.

And you can always ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy of it.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete something specific — including feedback or a submission you sent us.

Email elpysnotifications@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. We'll reply within 45 days and usually much sooner. We may need to confirm that you control the email address in question first, so that we're not handing your information to someone else. Asking never costs anything and we won't treat you differently for asking.

Elpys is small enough that it isn't currently covered by laws like the CCPA or the GDPR. We offer these rights anyway, to everyone, because they're the right thing to offer.

9. Security

What we do: the whole site is served over HTTPS and is protected by a strict content-security policy and related browser security headers. Passwords are hashed by Supabase; we never see them. Database access is locked down with row-level security, so one account can only ever read its own profile, and the tables holding feedback and internal review notes aren't publicly readable at all. The admin panel is password-protected and rate-limits guessing. Only the person who runs Elpys has administrative access.

What we can't promise: no website is perfectly secure, and Elpys is a one-person project, not a company with a security team. If a breach ever affects your information, we'll email you and tell you what happened, what to do about it, and what we're doing about it — within 30 days at the outside, as Washington law requires, and realistically much faster than that.

10. Where your information is handled

Elpys is run from the United States and every provider listed in section 6 processes data in the United States or in the region where it operates its own infrastructure. Elpys is built for people in the Bellevue, Washington area and isn't offered to people in the EU or UK. If you use it from outside the United States, your information will be handled in the United States.

11. If you're under 13 — and a note for parents and guardians

Elpys is intended for teens age 13 and older. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Creating an account requires confirming you're 13 or older. We don't ask anyone for an exact age or a date of birth, on this site or anywhere else.

Anyone can read the listings without giving us anything at all, and we'd rather a younger kid browse with a parent than be turned away — but please don't create an account, submit an opportunity, or send us feedback if you're under 13.

Parents and guardians: if you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email elpysnotifications@gmail.com and we will delete it and any associated account promptly, without requiring you to prove anything beyond enough detail to find the record. You can also ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about your child, or to stop collecting anything further.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page and note the change on the site. Elpys is small and actively developed, so it's worth checking back occasionally. We'll never apply a materially different use of information we already hold without telling you first.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, about what we hold, or about anything on this page? Email elpysnotifications@gmail.com. A real person reads it.

Listings are checked regularly for accuracy, but links and details can change — always confirm with the org before your first visit. Something look wrong? Let us know.
