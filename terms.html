Elpys tells you where opportunities are. Deciding whether a particular one is right for you is yours to make — and, if you're under 18, your parent's or guardian's too.

Before your first visit to any organization:

  • Confirm the details directly with the organization. Times, ages, locations, and requirements change.
  • Tell a parent, guardian, or another trusted adult where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Read and follow the organization's own rules, including any waiver, age minimum, orientation, or supervision requirement they set. Those are theirs, not ours, and they override anything a listing says.

Elpys does not supervise you, is not present, and is not responsible for what happens at an organization's site.
