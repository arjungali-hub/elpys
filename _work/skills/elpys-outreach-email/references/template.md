# Krish's outreach email template — organizations already listed on Elpys

Approved by Krish on Oct 11, 2026; wording changed at his request on Oct 13, 2026 (less certain "I checked" paragraph; "checked before it goes on the site"). Use it word for word. Only the bracketed
parts change. Do not reword, shorten, re-order or "improve" anything else —
he wrote this version himself and asked for it to be used exactly.

```
Subject: [Organization] is listed on a free directory for Bellevue teens

Hi there,

I'm Arjun, a student at Odle. I built Elpys, a free directory of volunteer opportunities that teens in the Bellevue area can sign up for on their own.

[Organization] is listed, and I wanted you to hear it from me. Your page is here: [link to opportunity]

I based the details on your own website, including the age minimum, whether an adult needs to be there, and the exact sign-up steps, but you know your program best. [One specific sentence about their program, e.g. "Your Saturday teen shifts are one of the few options open to 15-year-olds without a parent."]

Three things would help:

1. If anything on that page is wrong or out of date, just reply and I'll fix it within a week.
2. If a teen or parent ever asks where else they can volunteer, sharing elpys.vercel.app would mean a lot. It's free, has no ads, and every listing is checked before it goes on the site.
3. If you run other programs that take teen volunteers, you can add them at elpys.vercel.app/submit. I review every submission before it goes live.

Thanks for running a program that welcomes teen volunteers. There aren't many.

Arjun
Elpys · hello.elpys@gmail.com
```

## The three blanks

- **[Organization]** — appears twice (subject and body). Use the organization's
  own name as a person would say it, not the listing's full title. "Kelsey
  Creek Farm", "The Sophia Way" (not "The Sophia Way — Donation Center"),
  "Washington Trails Association".
- **[link to opportunity]** — `elpys.vercel.app/<slug>`, using the row's
  `slug` column. No `https://`, no trailing slash (trailing-slash URLs used
  to 404 on Elpys). Open it once in the browser to confirm it loads.
- **[One specific sentence…]** — one sentence, true today, about this
  specific program. See "Writing the specific sentence" in SKILL.md.
